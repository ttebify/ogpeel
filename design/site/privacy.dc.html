Privacy, in plain words.

Ogpeel looks at a page only when you click its icon. It keeps nothing about what you browse.

What the extension reads

The tags in the page you clicked on: the share image, title and description. Nothing from other tabs, and nothing in the background.

What it stores

Your settings and, if you paid, your key. Both stay in your browser. Uninstall Ogpeel and they're gone.

What it sends

Nothing while you use it. Once, when you enter a key, it asks our payment provider whether the key is real.

When you pay

Lemon Squeezy takes the payment and keeps your email and receipt, under its own privacy policy. We see your email and what you bought, so we can send your key again if you lose it.

Analytics

None in the extension. The website counts page visits without cookies.

Questions or deletion

Reply to any Ogpeel email and ask. We delete what we hold within 30 days.
