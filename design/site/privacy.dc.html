Privacy, in plain words.

Ogpeel looks at a page only when you click its icon. It keeps nothing about what you browse.

What the extension reads

Only the page you click Ogpeel on: its link preview images, title and description, the largest image when there is no preview image, and which framework built it. Nothing from other tabs, nothing in the background.

What it stores

Your settings, your chosen framework, how many free prompts are left and, if you paid, your key. All stay in your browser. Uninstall Ogpeel and they're gone.

What it sends

Nothing about you or the pages you visit. It loads the image from the site that hosts it, and checks your key on your device. Copy image may ask to access that one image site; you can say no.

When you pay

Bachs takes the payment, under its own privacy policy. Resend delivers the key email. We see your email and what you bought, so we can send your key again if you lose it.

Analytics

None in the extension. This website counts visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics, without cookies. The video loads from YouTube only when you play it.

Questions or deletion

Email hello@ogpeel.com. We delete what we hold within 30 days.
